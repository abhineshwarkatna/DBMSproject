📐 Database Normalization Architecture

Demonstrating how Eventora satisfies 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF to eliminate redundancy.

1️⃣ First Normal Form (1NF)

All attributes are atomic. No multi-valued repeating groups (e.g., guest lists or multiple vendor phones are separated into child tables).

guests table: Each row holds single guest record with atomic phone & email fields.

2️⃣ Second Normal Form (2NF)

No partial dependencies. Composite relationships between Events and Vendors are abstracted through the bookings junction table.

bookings: booking_id (PK), event_id (FK), vendor_id (FK), agreed_cost.

3️⃣ Third Normal Form (3NF)

No transitive dependencies (X → Y → Z). Non-key attributes like organizer phone or vendor ratings are not stored inside the events table.

events table only holds organizer_id (FK), referencing users independently.
